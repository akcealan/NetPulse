# Product Context

## Problem Statement

Students need a way to:

- Track their study sessions effectively
- Monitor their progress across different subjects
- Analyze their performance patterns
- Manage study time efficiently

## Solution

NetPulse provides:

1. Structured data entry for study sessions
2. Integrated timer functionality
3. Performance tracking
4. Statistical analysis of study patterns

## User Experience Goals

- Simple and intuitive navigation
- Quick data entry process
- Seamless timer integration
- Clear visualization of progress
- Easy access to historical data

## Target Users

- Students who want to track their study progress
- Learners who need to monitor their performance
- Users who want to analyze their study patterns
